# Griham — What we offer you

*A simple guide for families and households — no technical jargon.*

---

## In one sentence

**Griham helps your household see money, people, and day-to-day life in one place — so you can plan better, worry less, and stay organized together.**

---

## Who is Griham for?

- **Families** who want a shared picture of income, spending, bills, and cards  
- **Anyone** juggling multiple bank accounts, credit cards, and due dates  
- **Households** that want contacts and family details in one trusted place  
- **People** who’d like plain-language tips and reminders — not spreadsheets  

---

## What you get

### Your money, clearly laid out

- **Bank accounts** — See balances and what you have where.  
- **Income and spending** — Track what comes in and what goes out, by category and time.  
- **Bills** — Know what’s due, including recurring bills, so fewer surprises.  
- **Cards** — Credit and debit in one view — limits, billing dates, and status at a glance.  

*You stay in control: you choose what you enter and what you track.*

---

### Smarter help when you want it

Griham can offer **optional guidance** based on your own numbers — not generic advice from the internet:

- **Plain-English summaries** — “How did this month look?” in words you can actually use.  
- **Gentle risk reminders** — Flags that help you notice tight spots before they hurt.  
- **Savings and cash-flow ideas** — Suggestions tuned to *your* patterns.  
- **Easier search** — Ask in everyday language instead of clicking through filters.  
- **Bank SMS, made useful** — Turn text alerts from your bank into structured entries when you paste them — less typing, fewer mistakes.  

*If smart features aren’t turned on or aren’t available, the app still works for manual tracking.*

---

### Your household, not just your wallet

- **One place for the “family unit”** — Name, address, and the people who belong to it.  
- **Contacts** — Keep phone numbers and details organized; clean up duplicates and tidy your list with help when you want it.  

---

### The right people see the right things

- **Roles and access** — So adults, admins, or household managers can each see what they need — without everyone needing full access to everything.  
- **Root / admin setup** — For organizations or power users who define *who* can open finance, health-related modules, messages, and more.  

*Peace of mind: shared life, sensible boundaries.*

---

### One dashboard to start your day

- Quick view of **accounts, recent activity, bills, and cards**  
- Optional **insights** so you don’t have to read every line item to know how you’re doing

## What Griham is *not* (honest expectations)

- It is **not** a bank — we don’t move your money; you stay with your bank.  
- It is **not** a replacement for professional tax or legal advice.  
- **Smart features** depend on setup and availability — core tracking works either way.
